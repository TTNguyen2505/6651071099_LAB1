Artwork Provenance & Materials

Curated exclusively for ARTGANI, the Sienna Horizon Canvas captures the meditative tension between organic earth pigments and disciplined architectural form. Each gesture is laid down wet-into-wet with flexible palette knives, resulting in rich multi-layered sculptural ridges that cast dynamic micro-shadows as natural daylight shifts through your living space.

Every mineral pigment has been verified non-fugitive and acid-free, providing generational longevity rated at over 100 years under archival preservation standards. This piece arrives fully outfitted with pre-installed museum French cleats, solid brass hanging hardware, and an integrated spirit level to ensure effortless, millimeter-perfect home exhibition right out of the crate.
